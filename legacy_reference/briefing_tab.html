Why IDSS?

The National Weather Service is evolving from information provider to decision-support partner. Impact-Based Decision Support Services (IDSS) focus not just on what the weather is, but on what that weather means for infrastructure, operations, and public safety. By integrating likelihood and consequence, forecasters empower partners to make confident, timely decisions that save lives, protect property, and strengthen resilience to space-weather impacts.
